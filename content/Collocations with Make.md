Collocations are word combinations that sound natural together. With *make*, English often focuses on producing, creating, or causing something.

---

## Common collocations with make

- *make a decision*
- *make a mistake*
- *make progress*
- *make an effort*
- *make money*
- *make a plan*
- *make a noise*
- *make friends*

## Examples

- *We need to **make a decision** soon.*
- *She **made a mistake** in the report.*
- *They are **making progress** quickly.*
