*Borrow* and *lend* describe the same exchange from opposite directions.

---

## Basic difference

- *borrow* = take something from someone for a time
- *lend* = give something to someone for a time

## Examples

- *Can I **borrow** your pen?*
- *Could you **lend** me your pen?*

## Common pattern

- *borrow something from someone*
- *lend something to someone*
