Discourse markers are words and phrases that organize spoken or written communication. They help the listener or reader follow the flow of ideas.

---

## Common discourse markers

### Starting a point

- *well*
- *so*
- *right*

### Adding information

- *also*
- *besides*
- *in addition*

### Changing direction

- *however*
- *on the other hand*
- *anyway*

### Ordering ideas

- *firstly*
- *then*
- *finally*

## Example

*Well, I wanted to travel. However, the tickets were too expensive. So, in the end, I stayed at home.*
