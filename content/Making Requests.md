Making requests means asking someone to do something. Good request language often depends on how formal or polite you want to sound.

---

## Common request patterns

- *Can you ... ?*
- *Could you ... ?*
- *Would you mind ... ?*
- *Could I ... ?*

## Examples

- *Could you open the window, please?*
- *Would you mind helping me for a minute?*
- *Could I borrow your charger?*

## Polite additions

- *please*
- *if possible*
- *when you have a moment*
