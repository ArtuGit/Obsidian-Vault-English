*For*, *since*, and *ago* all relate to time, but they answer different questions.

---

## For

Use *for* with a length of time.

- *I have lived here **for** three years.*

## Since

Use *since* with a starting point.

- *She has worked here **since** 2022.*

## Ago

Use *ago* to count back from now.

- *He left two hours **ago**.*
