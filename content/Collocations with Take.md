*Take* collocates with actions involving movement, time, or deliberate action.

---

## Common collocations with take

- *take a photo*
- *take a break*
- *take notes*
- *take part*
- *take responsibility*
- *take care*
- *take a seat*

## Examples

- *Please **take a seat**.*
- *She **took part** in the competition.*
