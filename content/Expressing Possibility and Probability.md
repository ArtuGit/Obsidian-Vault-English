This language helps you say whether something is possible, probable, or uncertain.

---

## Common patterns

- *may*
- *might*
- *could*
- *probably*
- *perhaps / maybe*

## Examples

- *It might rain later.*
- *She will probably arrive soon.*
- *Maybe we took the wrong bus.*

## Stronger vs weaker certainty

- weaker: *might, maybe*
- stronger: *probably, must* for logical conclusion
