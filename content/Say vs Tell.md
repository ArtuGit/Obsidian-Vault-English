*Say* and *tell* both report speech, but their grammar is different.

---

## Say

Use *say* when the listener is not mentioned directly.

- *She said that she was tired.*
- *He said hello.*

## Tell

Use *tell* with an object.

- *She told me that she was tired.*
- *He told us a story.*

## Common mistake

- *She said me the answer* -> *She told me the answer.*
- *He told that he was late* -> *He said that he was late.*
