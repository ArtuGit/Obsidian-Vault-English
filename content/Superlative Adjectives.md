Superlative adjectives show that one person or thing has the highest or lowest degree in a group.

---

## Basic forms

- short adjectives: *the + adjective-est* -> *the tallest*
- longer adjectives: *the most + adjective* -> *the most comfortable*

Examples:

- *This is **the oldest** building in town.*
- *She is **the most patient** teacher I know.*

## Irregular forms

- *good* -> *the best*
- *bad* -> *the worst*
- *far* -> *the farthest / the furthest*

## Common patterns

- *the best in the class*
- *the most expensive of the three*
