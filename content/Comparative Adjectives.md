Comparative adjectives are used to compare two people, things, or situations.

---

## Basic forms

- short adjectives: add *-er* -> *smaller, faster*
- longer adjectives: use *more* -> *more interesting, more expensive*

Examples:

- *My bag is **smaller** than yours.*
- *This book is **more useful** than the other one.*

## Irregular forms

- *good* -> *better*
- *bad* -> *worse*
- *far* -> *farther/further*

## Common patterns

- *than*: *She is taller than me.*
- *a bit / much / far* + comparative: *much easier*, *far better*
