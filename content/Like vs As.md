*Like* and *as* can both compare things, but they do not usually work in the same structure.

---

## Like

Use *like* before a noun or pronoun.

- *He sings **like** a professional.*
- *It looks **like** rain.*

## As

Use *as* before a clause or job/role.

- *Do **as** I say.*
- *She works **as** a teacher.*

## Common mistake

- *Like I said* is common in conversation, but in careful grammar many people prefer *As I said*.
