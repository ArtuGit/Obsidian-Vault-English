Describing processes means explaining how something happens step by step. This is useful in exams, presentations, and everyday explanations.

---

## Useful language

- sequencing words: *first, next, then, after that, finally*
- passive voice: *is made, is mixed, is delivered*
- imperative forms for instructions: *add, press, wait*

## Example

*First, the beans are roasted. Next, they are ground. Then hot water is added. Finally, the coffee is served.*
