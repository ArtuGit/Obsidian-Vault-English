Making suggestions means proposing an idea or possible action. English has several useful patterns for this.

---

## Common patterns

- *Let's ...*
- *Why don't we ... ?*
- *How about ... ?*
- *What about ... ?*
- *We could ...*

## Examples

- *Let's go by train.*
- *Why don't we ask the teacher?*
- *How about meeting after lunch?*
- *We could try a different app.*

## Responding to suggestions

- *That sounds good.*
- *Good idea.*
- *I am not sure about that.*
