Indirect questions are more polite and less direct than normal questions. They often appear after expressions such as *Could you tell me ... ?* or *Do you know ... ?*

---

## Structure

The second part uses statement word order.

- Direct: *Where is the station?*
- Indirect: *Could you tell me where the station is?*

- Direct: *What time does the class start?*
- Indirect: *Do you know what time the class starts?*

## Common openings

- *Can you tell me ... ?*
- *Do you know ... ?*
- *Could you explain ... ?*
- *I wonder ...*

## Yes-no indirect questions

Use *if* or *whether*.

- *Do you know if the shop is open?*

## Common mistakes

- *Could you tell me where is the station?* -> *Could you tell me where the station is?*
