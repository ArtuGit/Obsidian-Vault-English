 Wh-questions ask for specific information such as a person, place, time, reason, or method.

---

## Common wh-words

- *who*
- *what*
- *where*
- *when*
- *why*
- *which*
- *how*

## Usual structure

- *Wh-word + auxiliary + subject + verb?*

Examples:

- *Where do you live?*
- *Why was she upset?*
- *How can I help?*

## Subject questions

When the wh-word is the subject, do not use *do/does/did*.

- *Who called you?*
- *What happened?*

Compare:

- *Who saw Anna?* (who = subject)
- *Who did Anna see?* (who = object)
