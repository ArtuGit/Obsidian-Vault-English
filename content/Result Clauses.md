Result clauses describe the consequence of something. They often show that one fact leads to another.

---

## Common patterns

### So ... that

- *The film was **so** funny **that** everyone laughed.*

### Such ... that

- *It was **such** a good book **that** I read it twice.*

### Too ... to

- *The box was **too** heavy **to** lift.*

### Enough to

- *She was calm **enough to** explain the problem.*

## So vs such

- *so + adjective/adverb*: *so tired*, *so quickly*
- *such + noun phrase*: *such a surprise*, *such good weather*
