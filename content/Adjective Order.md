When we use more than one adjective before a noun, English usually follows a preferred order. This helps sentences sound natural to native speakers.

---

## Common adjective order

The usual order is:

- opinion
- size
- age
- shape
- colour
- origin
- material
- purpose
- noun

Example:

- *a lovely small old round brown Italian wooden coffee table*

We do not always use all these categories, but when several adjectives appear together, this order is common.

## Useful examples

- *a beautiful red dress*
- *an old French film*
- *a large plastic box*
