Phrasal verbs with *get* are very common in everyday English. They often sound more natural than single-word verbs in conversation.

---

## Common examples

- *get up* = leave your bed
- *get back* = return
- *get along (with)* = have a good relationship
- *get over* = recover from
- *get by* = manage with difficulty

## Examples in sentences

- *I usually **get up** at 7.*
- *What time did you **get back**?*
- *She **gets along with** her colleagues.*
