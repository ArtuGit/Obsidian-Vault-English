*Remember* is about keeping something in your memory. *Remind* means helping another person remember something.

---

## Remember

- *I remembered her name.*
- *Remember to lock the door.*

## Remind

- *Please remind me about the meeting.*
- *This song reminds me of summer.*

## Common pattern

- *remind someone to do something*
- *remind someone of something*
