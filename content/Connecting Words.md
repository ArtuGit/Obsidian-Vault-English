Connecting words link ideas, clauses, and sentences. They help you show addition, contrast, reason, result, time, and examples more clearly.

---

## Common functions

### Addition

- *and*
- *also*
- *in addition*

### Contrast

- *but*
- *however*
- *although*

### Reason and result

- *because*
- *so*
- *therefore*

### Time and sequence

- *first*
- *then*
- *after that*
- *finally*

## Example paragraph

*I wanted to go out, but it started raining. So I stayed at home and finished my homework. After that, I watched a film.*
