Yes-no questions can be answered with *yes* or *no*. English usually forms them by putting an auxiliary verb before the subject.

---

## Basic structure

- *Auxiliary + subject + main verb?*

Examples:

- *Do you work here?*
- *Is she ready?*
- *Have they finished?*
- *Can he drive?*

## Questions with do/does/did

Use *do/does/did* when there is no other auxiliary.

- *Do you like tea?*
- *Does she play tennis?*
- *Did they arrive on time?*

The main verb stays in the base form.

- *Does he **work** here?*

## Short answers

- *Yes, I do.*
- *No, she isn't.*
- *Yes, they have.*
