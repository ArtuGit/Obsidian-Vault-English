*Make* and *do* are both common verbs, but they are used in different ways. A useful starting point is this: *make* often creates or produces something, while *do* often refers to tasks, work, or general activity.

---

## Use make when something is created or caused

- *make a cake*
- *make a mistake*
- *make a phone call*
- *make progress*

## Use do for tasks and activities

- *do homework*
- *do the cleaning*
- *do exercise*
- *do your best*
