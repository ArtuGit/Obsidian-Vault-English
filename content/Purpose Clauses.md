Purpose clauses explain why someone does something. They answer the question *for what purpose?*

---

## Common forms

### To-infinitive

- *She went to the shop **to buy** milk.*

### In order to / so as to

- *He left early **in order to** avoid traffic.*
- *We spoke quietly **so as not to** wake the baby.*

### So that

Use *so that* when the clause has its own subject or modal verb.

- *I wrote it down **so that** I wouldn't forget.*
- *They saved money **so that** they could travel.*
