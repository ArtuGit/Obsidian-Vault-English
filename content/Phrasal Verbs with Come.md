Phrasal verbs with *come* are common for movement, ideas, and events.

---

## Common examples

- *come back* = return
- *come in* = enter
- *come from* = originate in
- *come across* = find by chance
- *come up with* = think of an idea

## Examples in sentences

- *She **came back** yesterday.*
- *I **came across** an old photo.*
- *He **came up with** a clever solution.*
