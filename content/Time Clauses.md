Time clauses show when something happens. They are usually introduced by words such as *when, before, after, while, until,* and *as soon as*.

---

## Common time-clause words

- *when*
- *before*
- *after*
- *while*
- *until / till*
- *as soon as*
- *once*

## Examples

- *Call me **when** you get home.*
- *I brushed my teeth **before** I went to bed.*
- *She was listening to music **while** she was cooking.*

## Future meaning

After time words, English often uses the present tense for future meaning.

- *I will text you **when I arrive**.*
- *We will leave **as soon as the rain stops**.*
