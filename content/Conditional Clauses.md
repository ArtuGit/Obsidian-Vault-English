Conditional clauses describe the condition for another action or situation. They are often introduced by *if*, but English also uses words such as *unless*, *provided*, and *as long as*.

---

## Basic structure

- *If + condition, result*
- *Result + if + condition*

Examples:

- *If it rains, we will stay inside.*
- *I will call you if I arrive early.*

## Common clause markers

- *if*
- *unless* = except if
- *provided (that)*
- *as long as*
- *in case*

## Important tense point

When the condition refers to the future, English often uses a present form after *if*, not *will*.

- *If I **see** her, I will tell her.*
- Not: *If I will see her ...*
