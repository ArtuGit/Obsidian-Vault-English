Contrast clauses show an unexpected difference between two ideas. They are useful when comparing facts that do not seem to match.

---

## Common connectors

- *although / though / even though*
- *while*
- *whereas*
- *however*

## Examples

- *Although it was late, we kept working.*
- *She went out even though she felt tired.*
- *I like tea, whereas my brother prefers coffee.*

## Clause vs linker

Words such as *although* and *whereas* introduce a clause.

- *Although it was raining, we walked home.*

Words such as *however* often link separate clauses or sentences.

- *It was raining. However, we walked home.*
