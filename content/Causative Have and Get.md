Causative *have* and *get* describe situations where another person does something for us or because of us. They are common when talking about services and arrangements.

---

## Basic structure

### Have/Get + object + past participle

- *I **had my hair cut** yesterday.*
- *She **got her phone repaired**.*

This structure means someone else did the action.

## Common uses

### Services

- *We had the house painted.*
- *They got the car washed.*

### Arrangements

- *I had the documents printed before the meeting.*

### Unwanted events

- *He had his wallet stolen.*

## Have vs get

Both are correct. *Get* is usually more informal and common in speech.

## Other causative patterns

- *have someone do something*: *I had the assistant send the email.*
- *get someone to do something*: *She got her brother to help.*
