*Too* and *enough* both talk about degree, but they point in opposite directions.

---

## Too

Use *too* when something is more than necessary or more than wanted.

- *The soup is **too** hot to eat.*
- *She spoke **too** fast.*

## Enough

Use *enough* when something is sufficient.

- *The soup is cool **enough** to eat.*
- *He is old **enough** to drive.*

## Position

- *too* comes before the adjective or adverb
- *enough* comes after the adjective or adverb
