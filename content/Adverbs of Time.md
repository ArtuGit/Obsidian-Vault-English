Adverbs of time tell us when, how often in sequence, or for how long something happens.

---

## Common examples

- *now*
- *today*
- *soon*
- *already*
- *yet*
- *recently*
- *later*

## Examples in sentences

- *I will call you **later**.*
- *She has **already** finished.*
- *Have you eaten **yet**?*

## Position

Many adverbs of time go at the end of the clause, but some common ones such as *already* and *yet* have more fixed positions.
