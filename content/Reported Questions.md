Reported questions tell us what someone asked, but they do not keep the direct question form.

---

## Yes-no questions

Use *if* or *whether*.

- Direct: *"Do you like coffee?"*
- Reported: *She asked if I liked coffee.*

## Wh-questions

Keep the question word.

- Direct: *"Where do you live?"*
- Reported: *He asked where I lived.*

## Word order

Reported questions use statement word order, not question word order.

- *She asked where I lived.*
- Not: *She asked where did I live.*

There is also no *do/does/did* in the reported clause.

## Polite reporting verbs

Common verbs are:

- *ask*
- *want to know*
- *wonder*
