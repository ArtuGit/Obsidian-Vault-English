Adjective formation shows how English creates adjectives from nouns, verbs, or other word bases. This helps you expand vocabulary more systematically.

---

## Common adjective suffixes

- *-ful*: *careful, useful*
- *-less*: *careless, hopeless*
- *-ous*: *dangerous, famous*
- *-ive*: *active, creative*
- *-al*: *natural, cultural*

## Prefixes with adjectives

Prefixes can also change adjective meaning.

- *happy* -> *unhappy*
- *regular* -> *irregular*
- *possible* -> *impossible*
