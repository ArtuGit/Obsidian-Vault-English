Reporting verbs introduce what someone said, asked, advised, promised, or thought. They are more varied than basic *say* and *tell*.

---

## Common groups

### Statement verbs

- *say*
- *tell*
- *explain*
- *admit*
- *deny*

### Question verbs

- *ask*
- *wonder*
- *want to know*

### Command and advice verbs

- *tell*
- *ask*
- *advise*
- *warn*
- *remind*
- *order*

### Promise and plan verbs

- *promise*
- *offer*
- *refuse*
- *agree*

## Different patterns

- *say + clause*: *She said she was tired.*
- *tell + object + clause*: *He told me he was leaving.*
- *advise + object + to-infinitive*: *They advised us to wait.*
- *suggest + gerund / clause*: *She suggested going early.*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do not use *tell* without an object: *He told me...*, not *He told that...*
