*Get* collocates with change, movement, and receiving or becoming something.

---

## Common collocations with get

- *get ready*
- *get married*
- *get dressed*
- *get better*
- *get lost*
- *get a job*
- *get permission*

## Examples

- *We need to **get ready** now.*
- *He is slowly **getting better**.*
