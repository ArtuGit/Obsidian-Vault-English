Compound nouns are nouns made from two or more words that function together as one idea.

---

## Common patterns

- noun + noun: *bus stop, coffee shop*
- adjective + noun: *blackboard, greenhouse*
- verb + noun: *swimming pool, washing machine*

## Writing style

Compound nouns can be written:

- as one word: *toothbrush*
- as two words: *credit card*
- with a hyphen: *mother-in-law*

There is not always one simple rule, so learners often need to notice common usage.
