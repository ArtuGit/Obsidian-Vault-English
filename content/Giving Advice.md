Giving advice means telling someone what you think is a good idea in a situation.

---

## Common advice patterns

- *You should ...*
- *You should not ...*
- *You ought to ...*
- *If I were you, I would ...*
- *It is a good idea to ...*

## Examples

- *You should talk to your manager.*
- *If I were you, I would take a short break.*
- *It is a good idea to check the instructions first.*
