*May* and *might* are modal verbs used mainly for possibility and permission. *Might* usually sounds a little less certain than *may*.

---

## Possibility

- *It **may** rain later.*
- *She **might** join us after work.*

The difference is often small, but *might* can sound more tentative.

## Permission

- *You **may** leave now.*
- *May I ask a question?*

In modern spoken English, *can* is often used instead of *may* for permission.

## Perfect forms

Use *may have* and *might have* to talk about past possibility.

- *He **may have forgotten** the meeting.*
- *They **might have taken** the wrong train.*

## Common mistakes

- *Maybe he comes later* is natural, but with a modal use *He may come later.*
- *May* for permission can sound formal in some situations.
