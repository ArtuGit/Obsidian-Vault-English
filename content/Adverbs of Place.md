Adverbs of place tell us where an action happens or in what direction something moves.

---

## Common examples

- *here*
- *there*
- *inside*
- *outside*
- *everywhere*
- *abroad*
- *upstairs*

## Examples

- *Please sit **here**.*
- *The children are playing **outside**.*
- *We looked **everywhere** for the keys.*

## Position

Adverbs of place usually come after the main verb or object.

- *She put the bag **there**.*
