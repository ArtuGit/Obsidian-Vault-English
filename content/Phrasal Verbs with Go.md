Phrasal verbs with *go* are especially common in informal English.

---

## Common examples

- *go on* = continue / happen
- *go out* = leave home for social activity / stop burning
- *go back* = return
- *go off* = explode / ring / no longer like
- *go through* = experience / examine carefully

## Examples in sentences

- *Please **go on** with your story.*
- *The lights **went out** during the storm.*
- *She has **gone through** a difficult year.*
