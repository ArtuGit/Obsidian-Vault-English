Phrasal verbs with *make* are less common than some other phrasal-verb groups, but several are useful at B1 level.

---

## Common examples

- *make up* = invent / become friends again / form
- *make out* = understand with difficulty
- *make for* = move towards / cause
- *make up for* = compensate for
- *make off* = escape quickly, usually after a crime

## Examples in sentences

- *He **made up** an excuse.*
- *I could not **make out** the sign in the dark.*
- *She is trying to **make up for** the delay.*
