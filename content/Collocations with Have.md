*Have* collocates with experiences, possession, meals, and many everyday events.

---

## Common collocations with have

- *have breakfast / lunch / dinner*
- *have a shower*
- *have a look*
- *have fun*
- *have a problem*
- *have a rest*
- *have an idea*

## Examples

- *Let's **have a look** at the map.*
- *We **had dinner** at eight.*
