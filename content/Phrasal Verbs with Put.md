Phrasal verbs with *put* are frequent in practical and everyday contexts.

---

## Common examples

- *put on* = dress in / switch on
- *put off* = delay
- *put away* = return to its place
- *put out* = extinguish
- *put up with* = tolerate

## Examples in sentences

- *She **put on** her coat.*
- *They **put off** the meeting until Friday.*
- *I cannot **put up with** that noise any longer.*
