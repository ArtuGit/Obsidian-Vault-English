Expressing opinions means saying what you think, believe, or feel about a topic. At B1 level, learners need a range from simple direct phrases to slightly softer, more careful ones.

---

## Useful phrases

- *I think ...*
- *I believe ...*
- *In my opinion, ...*
- *From my point of view, ...*
- *It seems to me that ...*

## Example sentences

- *I think online learning is useful.*
- *In my opinion, public transport should be cheaper.*
- *It seems to me that this film is too long.*

## Softer opinions

If you want to sound less direct, use:

- *I guess ...*
- *I would say ...*
- *It might be better to ...*
