*Already*, *yet*, and *still* are common adverbs that relate to time and expectation.

---

## Already

Use *already* for something that happened earlier than expected.

- *I have **already** finished.*

## Yet

Use *yet* mainly in negatives and questions.

- *I have not finished **yet**.*
- *Have you eaten **yet**?*

## Still

Use *still* for a situation that continues.

- *She is **still** working.*
- *Do you **still** live there?*
