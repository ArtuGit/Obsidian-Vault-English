Reported commands and requests tell us what someone told, asked, or ordered another person to do.

---

## Main pattern

Use:

- *reporting verb + object + to-infinitive*

Examples:

- *The teacher told us to open our books.*
- *She asked me to wait outside.*
- *The officer ordered them to stop.*

## Negative commands

Use *not to*.

- *Dad told me not to be late.*
- *She asked him not to touch the painting.*

## Common reporting verbs

- *tell*
- *ask*
- *order*
- *advise*
- *warn*
- *remind*
