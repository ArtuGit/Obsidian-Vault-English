A gerund is the *-ing* form of a verb used like a noun. Gerunds are common after certain verbs, after prepositions, and as subjects or objects.

---

## Main uses

### As a subject

- *Reading helps me relax.*
- *Travelling can be expensive.*

### After certain verbs

- *I enjoy **reading**.*
- *They finished **cleaning**.*
- *She avoided **answering** the question.*

### After prepositions

- *She is good at **explaining** things.*
- *Thank you for **helping** me.*

## Common verbs followed by gerunds

- *enjoy*
- *finish*
- *avoid*
- *suggest*
- *mind*
- *keep*
