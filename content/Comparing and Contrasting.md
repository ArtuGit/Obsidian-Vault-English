Comparing and contrasting language helps you show similarities and differences between ideas, people, or situations.

---

## Comparing

- *both*
- *similarly*
- *in the same way*
- comparatives: *bigger than, more useful than*

## Contrasting

- *but*
- *however*
- *whereas*
- *on the other hand*

## Example

*City life is more convenient, whereas country life is usually quieter.*
