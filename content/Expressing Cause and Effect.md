Cause-and-effect language explains why something happens and what result follows.

---

## Common cause expressions

- *because*
- *because of*
- *due to*
- *as a result of*

## Common effect expressions

- *so*
- *therefore*
- *as a result*
- *that is why*

## Example

*The roads were icy, so many people stayed at home.*
