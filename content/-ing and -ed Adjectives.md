Adjectives ending in *-ing* usually describe the thing that causes a feeling, while adjectives ending in *-ed* usually describe the person who feels it.

---

## Main contrast

- *The lesson was **boring**.*
- *The students were **bored**.*

- *The news is **exciting**.*
- *We are **excited**.*

## Quick rule

- *-ing* = causes the feeling
- *-ed* = experiences the feeling

## Common pairs

- *interesting / interested*
- *confusing / confused*
- *annoying / annoyed*
- *surprising / surprised*

> [!TIP]
> If the noun cannot feel emotion, the *-ing* form is usually the right choice.
