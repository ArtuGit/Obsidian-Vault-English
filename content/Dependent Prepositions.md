Some adjectives, verbs, and nouns are regularly followed by specific prepositions. These combinations are called dependent prepositions.

---

## Common adjective + preposition patterns

- *good at*
- *interested in*
- *afraid of*
- *responsible for*

## Common verb + preposition patterns

- *listen to*
- *belong to*
- *depend on*
- *apologise for*

## Common noun + preposition patterns

- *reason for*
- *answer to*
- *difference between*

> [!NOTE]
> Dependent prepositions usually need to be learned as fixed combinations.
