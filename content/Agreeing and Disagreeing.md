Agreeing and disagreeing language helps you respond to other people's ideas politely and clearly.

---

## Agreeing

- *I agree.*
- *Exactly.*
- *That's true.*
- *I think so too.*
- *You are absolutely right.*

## Disagreeing politely

- *I am not sure I agree.*
- *I see your point, but ...*
- *I do not think that is quite right.*
- *Actually, I think ...*

## Example exchange

- *A: I think exams are useful.*
- *B: I see your point, but I think projects are more practical.*
