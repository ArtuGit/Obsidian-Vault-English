Adverbs of manner describe how an action happens. Many are formed by adding *-ly* to an adjective.

---

## Common examples

- *slowly*
- *carefully*
- *quietly*
- *badly*
- *well*

## Position

Adverbs of manner often come after the verb or after the object.

- *She spoke **quietly**.*
- *He opened the door **carefully**.*

## Irregular example

- adjective: *good*
- adverb: *well*
