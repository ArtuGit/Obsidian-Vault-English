Suffixes are groups of letters added to the end of a word. They often change the word class, for example from a verb to a noun or from a noun to an adjective.

---

## Common suffixes

| Suffix | Typical result | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -er / -or | person / thing | *teacher, actor* |
| -ness | noun | *kindness, darkness* |
| -ment | noun | *development, agreement* |
| -ful | adjective | *helpful, careful* |
| -less | adjective | *hopeless, careless* |
| -ly | adverb | *slowly, politely* |

## Useful point

Some suffixes change spelling.

- *happy* -> *happiness*
- *create* -> *creative*
- *use* -> *useful*
