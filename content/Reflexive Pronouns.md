Reflexive pronouns show that the subject and object are the same person or thing. They are also used for emphasis in English.

---

## Forms

| Subject | Reflexive pronoun |
| --- | --- |
| I | myself |
| you | yourself / yourselves |
| he | himself |
| she | herself |
| it | itself |
| we | ourselves |
| they | themselves |

## Main uses

### 1. Same subject and object

- *She taught **herself** Spanish.*
- *I hurt **myself** while cooking.*

### 2. For emphasis

- *The manager **herself** answered the email.*
- *We painted the room **ourselves**.*

### 3. With fixed expressions

- *Help yourself.*
- *Behave yourself.*
- *Enjoy yourself.*

## Reflexive vs reciprocal meaning

Use a reflexive pronoun when one person acts on the same person.

- *He blamed **himself**.*

Use *each other* or *one another* when two or more people act on one another.

- *They blamed **each other**.*

## Common mistakes

- *She introduced myself.* -> *She introduced herself.*
- *We enjoyed us.* -> *We enjoyed ourselves.*
- *They looked at themselves* is different from *They looked at each other*.
