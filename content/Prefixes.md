Prefixes are groups of letters added to the beginning of a word to change its meaning. They often create opposites, repetition, degree, or negative meaning.

---

## Common prefixes

| Prefix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| un- | not / opposite | *unhappy, unfair* |
| re- | again | *rewrite, return* |
| dis- | opposite / not | *disagree, disappear* |
| mis- | wrongly / badly | *misunderstand, misplace* |
| im-/in-/ir-/il- | not | *impossible, indirect, irregular, illegal* |

## Why prefixes matter

Learning prefixes helps you guess the meaning of unfamiliar words.

- *possible* -> *impossible*
- *appear* -> *disappear*
- *use* -> *reuse*
