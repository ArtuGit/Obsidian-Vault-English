Describing habits means talking about actions that happen regularly or repeatedly.

---

## Common language

- present simple: *I usually walk to work.*
- adverbs of frequency: *always, often, sometimes, rarely*
- expressions of routine: *every day, on weekends, once a week*

## Examples

- *She often studies in the evening.*
- *We usually eat together on Sundays.*
- *He rarely watches TV.*
