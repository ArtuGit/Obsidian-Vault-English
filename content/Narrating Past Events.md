Narrating past events means telling a story or describing something that happened before now.

---

## Useful language

- past simple for main events
- past continuous for background actions
- past perfect for earlier past events
- sequencing words: *first, then, after that, finally*

## Example

*First, we arrived at the station. Then we realised we had forgotten the tickets. While we were looking for them, the train left.*
