Phrasal verbs with *take* are common in work, travel, and everyday routines.

---

## Common examples

- *take off* = remove / leave the ground
- *take up* = start a hobby or activity
- *take on* = accept responsibility
- *take after* = be similar to a family member
- *take back* = return something or admit a statement was wrong

## Examples in sentences

- *Please **take off** your shoes.*
- *She **took up** yoga last year.*
- *He really **takes after** his father.*
