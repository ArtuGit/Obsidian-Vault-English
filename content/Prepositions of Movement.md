Prepositions of movement describe direction or motion from one place to another.

---

## Common prepositions

- *to*
- *into*
- *out of*
- *towards*
- *across*
- *through*
- *along*
- *up / down*

## Examples

- *She walked **into** the room.*
- *They ran **across** the street.*
- *We drove **through** the tunnel.*
