Some verbs are followed by a gerund, some by an infinitive, and some can take both with little or big changes in meaning.

---

## Verb + gerund

- *enjoy doing*
- *avoid doing*
- *suggest doing*

## Verb + infinitive

- *want to do*
- *decide to do*
- *hope to do*

## Verbs with a meaning change

### Remember

- *remember to do* = remember before the action
- *remember doing* = remember the memory of the action

### Stop

- *stop to do* = stop one action in order to do another
- *stop doing* = finish the activity

### Try

- *try to do* = make an effort
- *try doing* = test an idea as an experiment
