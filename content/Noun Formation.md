Noun formation is the process of making nouns from verbs, adjectives, or other nouns. It is useful for vocabulary building and academic writing.

---

## Common patterns

### Verb -> noun

- *develop* -> *development*
- *arrive* -> *arrival*
- *decide* -> *decision*

### Adjective -> noun

- *kind* -> *kindness*
- *active* -> *activity*
- *dark* -> *darkness*

### Person nouns

- *teach* -> *teacher*
- *act* -> *actor*
- *employ* -> *employee*
