Adverbs can also have comparative and superlative forms to compare how actions happen.

---

## Comparative adverbs

- *faster*
- *more carefully*
- *better*
- *worse*

Examples:

- *She drives **more carefully** than I do.*
- *He ran **faster** than his brother.*

## Superlative adverbs

- *the fastest*
- *the most clearly*
- *the best*
- *the worst*

Examples:

- *Of all the students, Mina speaks **the most clearly**.*
