*Bring* and *take* both describe carrying something, but the difference depends on direction.

---

## Basic idea

- *bring* = movement toward the speaker or destination
- *take* = movement away from the speaker or starting point

## Examples

- *Can you **bring** your laptop to the meeting?*
- *I will **take** these books back to the library.*

## Memory tip

Think about the point of view.

- *Bring it here.*
- *Take it there.*
