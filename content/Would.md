*Would* is a modal verb used for polite language, past habits, imagined situations, and conditional meaning. It often appears in everyday conversation and in [[Conditionals]].

---

## Main uses

### 1. Polite requests and offers

- *Would you help me with this bag?*
- *Would you like some tea?*

### 2. Imagined situations

- *I **would travel** more if I had time.*
- *She **would be** a great manager.*

### 3. Future in the past

- *He said he **would call** later.*
- *We knew the weather **would change**.*

### 4. Past habits

*Would* can describe repeated past actions, especially in stories.

- *Every summer, we **would visit** our grandparents.*

Use *used to* instead when talking about past states.

- *I **used to** live near the sea.*

## Common patterns

- *would like to* for polite wants
- *would rather* for preference
- *would you mind ... ?* for polite requests

## Common mistakes

- *I would can help* -> *I could help* / *I would be able to help*
- *When I was a child, I would be shy* is not natural for a state. Use *used to be shy*.
