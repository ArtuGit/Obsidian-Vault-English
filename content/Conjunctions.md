Conjunctions are words that connect words, phrases, or clauses. They help build longer and clearer sentences.

---

## Main types

### Coordinating conjunctions

These join equal parts.

- *and*
- *but*
- *or*
- *so*

Examples:

- *I called him, but he did not answer.*
- *We can stay home or go out.*

### Subordinating conjunctions

These introduce dependent clauses.

- *because*
- *if*
- *although*
- *when*

Examples:

- *I stayed home because I was tired.*
- *If it rains, we will cancel the picnic.*
