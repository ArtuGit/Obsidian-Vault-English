Adverbs of degree show intensity. They tell us how much, how strongly, or to what extent something is true.

---

## Common adverbs of degree

- *very*
- *really*
- *quite*
- *too*
- *enough*
- *almost*
- *hardly*

## Examples

- *The test was **very** difficult.*
- *I am **quite** tired.*
- *It is **too** late to call.*
- *She was **almost** ready.*

## Important contrasts

- *too* = more than necessary
- *enough* = sufficient amount
- *hardly* = almost not
