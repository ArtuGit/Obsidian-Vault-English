Prepositions of time show when something happens. The most common ones at B1 level are *at, on,* and *in*.

---

## At

Use *at* for exact times and some fixed expressions.

- *at 7 o'clock*
- *at noon*
- *at night*

## On

Use *on* for days and dates.

- *on Monday*
- *on 12 May*
- *on my birthday*

## In

Use *in* for months, years, parts of the day, and longer periods.

- *in July*
- *in 2026*
- *in the morning*
