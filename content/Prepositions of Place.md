Prepositions of place show the position of something. They answer the question *where?*

---

## Common prepositions

- *in* = inside
- *on* = touching a surface
- *at* = a point or location
- *under*
- *behind*
- *between*
- *next to*

## Examples

- *The keys are **on** the table.*
- *She is waiting **at** the bus stop.*
- *The cat is **under** the chair.*
