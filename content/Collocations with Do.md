*Do* often collocates with tasks, activities, and general actions rather than creating or producing something.

---

## Common collocations with do

- *do homework*
- *do housework*
- *do exercise*
- *do business*
- *do your best*
- *do the washing-up*
- *do a course*

## Examples

- *I have to **do my homework** tonight.*
- *He always **does his best** in exams.*
