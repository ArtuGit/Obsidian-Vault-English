Phrasal verbs with *look* often relate to searching, care, or direction.

---

## Common examples

- *look for* = try to find
- *look after* = take care of
- *look at* = direct your eyes towards
- *look up* = search for information / improve
- *look forward to* = feel excited about a future event

## Examples in sentences

- *I am **looking for** my keys.*
- *Can you **look after** the children tonight?*
- *We **look forward to** hearing from you.*
