*So* and *such* are both used for emphasis, especially before result clauses, but their grammar is different.

---

## So

Use *so* before an adjective or adverb.

- *The test was **so** difficult.*
- *She spoke **so** quietly.*

## Such

Use *such* before a noun phrase.

- *It was **such** a difficult test.*
- *They are **such** friendly people.*
