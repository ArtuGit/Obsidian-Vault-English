An infinitive is the base form of a verb. It often appears with *to*, but some structures use the bare infinitive without *to*.

---

## To-infinitive

Use the to-infinitive for many common patterns.

- *I want **to leave** now.*
- *She decided **to study** medicine.*
- *It is important **to listen** carefully.*

## Bare infinitive

Use the bare infinitive after modal verbs and some verbs such as *make* and *let*.

- *You must **leave** now.*
- *They made us **wait** outside.*
- *Let him **finish**.*

## Common uses

- purpose: *to learn more*
- after adjectives: *happy to help*
- after question words: *what to do, how to begin*
