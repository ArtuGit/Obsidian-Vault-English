Reported statements tell us what someone said without using their exact words. This is also called indirect speech.

---

## Basic pattern

The most common structure is:

- *subject + reporting verb + that-clause*

Examples:

- Direct: *"I am tired."*
- Reported: *She said (that) she was tired.*

The word *that* is often optional.

## Backshift of tense

When the reporting verb is in the past, the tense often moves back.

| Direct speech | Reported speech |
| --- | --- |
| present simple | past simple |
| present continuous | past continuous |
| present perfect | past perfect |
| will | would |

- *"I work here."* -> *He said he worked there.*
- *"I will call you."* -> *She said she would call me.*

## Changes in pronouns and time words

- *I* -> *he/she*
- *my* -> *his/her*
- *today* -> *that day*
- *tomorrow* -> *the next day*
- *here* -> *there*

## When backshift is not necessary

Backshift is often optional when the information is still true.

- *The teacher said that the Earth moves around the Sun.*
