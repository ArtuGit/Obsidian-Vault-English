Phrasal verbs with *break* are often used for machines, relationships, crime, and sudden events.

---

## Common examples

- *break down* = stop working / become very emotional
- *break up* = end a relationship / separate into smaller parts
- *break out* = start suddenly
- *break in* = enter by force / interrupt
- *break into* = enter a place by force

## Examples in sentences

- *Our car **broke down** on the motorway.*
- *A fire **broke out** in the kitchen.*
- *Someone **broke into** the shop last night.*
