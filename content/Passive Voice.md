The passive voice changes the focus of a sentence from the doer of an action to the action or result. It is common when the doer is unknown, obvious, or less important.

---

## Form

The passive is formed with **be + past participle**.

- *The window **was broken**.*
- *English **is spoken** in many countries.*
- *The report **has been finished**.*

## When we use the passive

### 1. The doer is unknown

- *My bike **was stolen** last night.*

### 2. The action is more important than the doer

- *The bridge **was opened** in 2010.*

### 3. Formal, scientific, or process language

- *The samples **are tested** in a lab.*

## By-phrases

Use *by* when the doer is important.

- *The song was written **by Adele**.*

Leave it out when it adds little information.

- *The office is cleaned every evening.*

## Active vs passive

- Active: *The chef cooked the meal.*
- Passive: *The meal was cooked by the chef.*
