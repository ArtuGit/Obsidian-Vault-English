Phrasal verbs with *bring* often relate to carrying, introducing topics, or causing change.

---

## Common examples

- *bring back* = return something / make someone remember
- *bring up* = raise a child / mention a topic
- *bring about* = cause
- *bring in* = introduce / earn
- *bring out* = publish / highlight

## Examples in sentences

- *These photos **bring back** old memories.*
- *She **brought up** an important question.*
- *The new policy **brought about** several changes.*
