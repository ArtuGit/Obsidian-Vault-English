This language is used when something is required, necessary, or strongly expected.

---

## Common patterns

- *must*
- *have to*
- *need to*
- *be required to*

## Examples

- *You must wear your ID badge.*
- *I have to finish this today.*
- *We need to leave now.*

## Useful contrast

- *must not* = prohibition
- *do not have to* = no obligation
