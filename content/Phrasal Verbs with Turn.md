Phrasal verbs with *turn* often describe movement, change, or controlling devices.

---

## Common examples

- *turn on* = start a device
- *turn off* = stop a device
- *turn up* = arrive / increase volume
- *turn down* = reduce volume / reject
- *turn into* = become something else

## Examples in sentences

- *Could you **turn down** the music?*
- *He **turned up** late again.*
- *The village **turned into** a busy town.*
